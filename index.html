Sports · Mobile app

Lucentum — Fan app for a Spanish sports club

A sports club app that puts match days, tickets, memberships and the club store in every fan's pocket.

Overview

Lucentum is a mobile app for a sports team in Spain. It combines everything a supporter needs — events, matches and teams — with ticketing and a full e-commerce and payment flow.

Under the hood it covers the whole journey: authentication and onboarding, localisation, push notifications, user profiles, and a store with cart, checkout, orders and memberships.

Highlights

  • Authentication & onboarding — login, token persistence, splash-screen routing, onboarding flow and localisation.
  • Sports & events — events, matches, teams and tickets.
  • Club store — products, cart, wishlist, recommendations, orders, checkout and payments.
  • Memberships — sold and managed in the same payment flow as the store.
  • Profiles — user information, avatar, bio and contact details that can be edited in-app.
  • Push notifications — Firebase Cloud Messaging wired in from first launch.

Maps · Mobile app

Pirata — Live location tracking & route guidance

A location-first app with real-time tracking, interactive maps and optimal scheduled routes across multiple store visits.

Overview

Pirata is built around the map. It tracks the user's position in real time, plans the optimal route across a day of scheduled stops, and guides them from one to the next.

Dynamic markers surface nearby stores and points of interest, and each visit opens into a step-by-step task flow.

Highlights

  • Real-time location tracking — the user's position stays accurate while walking or driving.
  • Optimal scheduled routes — a day of visits ordered into the most efficient path, with time and distance.
  • Live route guidance — directions that update as the user moves.
  • Dynamic markers — nearby stores and points of interest appear on the map as it moves.
  • Interactive maps — pan, zoom and tap straight into the details of each stop.

Blockchain · Final-year project

Rigel — Blockchain freelancing platform

An Upwork-style marketplace where project terms and payments are enforced by Ethereum smart contracts — with built-in conflict resolution.

Overview

Rigel was my final-year project: a freelancing platform that replaces trust in a middleman with smart contracts. Project terms, milestones and payments are encoded on-chain and enforced automatically.

Freelancers and clients each get their own dashboard for postings, proposals, orders and progress, and a dedicated flow resolves disputes transparently.

Highlights

  • Smart-contract agreements — deliverables and payment schedules are self-executing, removing payment disputes.
  • Tamper-proof records — project descriptions and milestones are recorded immutably.
  • Conflict resolution — a structured, transparent process when the two sides disagree.
  • Two-sided dashboards — separate client and freelancer experiences in one app.
